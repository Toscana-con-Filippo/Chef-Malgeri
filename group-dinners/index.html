Service notes

Simple details, arranged privately.

Wine — Paired wines are included and adjusted according to the guest count.

Travel — Chef travel to the villa is handled as a single €50 extra fee, not per person.

Payment — Cash follows the listed price. Card payment carries a 4% processing surcharge.

Requests — Timing, allergies and dietary requests are confirmed before booking.
