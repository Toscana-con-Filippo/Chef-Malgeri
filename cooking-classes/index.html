Chef MalgeriCooking Classes

Hands-on Tuscan cooking lessons at the villa, followed by a relaxed tasting with wine and printed recipes.

For guests who want to cook together without turning the day into logistics: ingredients, guidance and timing are arranged through Filippo Severi.

Hands-on lessons

Each class is compact, convivial and designed around dishes guests can actually prepare. The experience is kept small, clear and easy to arrange for a villa stay.
